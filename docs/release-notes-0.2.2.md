# Scene 0.2.2 (alpha)

A hotfix for Scene 0.2.1.

**This is still an alpha.** Expect rough edges and changes between releases. Export your settings before you update, and report anything that breaks in Issues.

### Fixes
- Down from the Spotlight buttons stops at the Continue watching and Coming up tabs instead of skipping them
- Air times on Coming up cards sit on a dark chip, so they read on bright art

### Changes
- Your library and Newly popular are separate rows again
- Newly popular has its own See all with every title in the window, not only the most popular share the row shows. Filter by films, series or anime, the time window, the order and seen titles. The header slides away while you scroll
- Coming up is off by default. Turn it on in Settings, Coming Up
