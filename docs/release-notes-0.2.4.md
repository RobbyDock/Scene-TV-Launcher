# Scene 0.2.4 (alpha)

A small update to Scene 0.2.3.

**This is still an alpha.** Expect rough edges and changes between releases. Export your settings before you update, and report anything that breaks in Issues.

### Changes
- Coming up is a row of its own above Your library again, not a tab beside Continue watching
- Newly popular is removed, along with its settings
- The Play, Resume and Get Stremio buttons no longer glow
- The update dot on the settings button is no longer cut by the focus ring
- The Coming Up preview in Settings shows two cards at Home's size, so nothing clips
- The credits line in About and the line at the foot of the Updates pane are gone. Update progress and problems show as a toast
- The launcher icon button moved out of the Apps list. It shows in About only while Demo Mode is on, and it is off by default
