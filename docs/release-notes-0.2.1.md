# Scene 0.2.1 (alpha)

A polish release for Scene, a calm, ad-free home screen for Android TV and Google TV. It adds Coming up and Newly popular to Home, a new screensaver and more ways to make Home your own.

**This is still an alpha.** Expect rough edges and changes between releases. Export your settings before you update, and report anything that breaks in Issues.

### Home
- Coming up: the next episodes of the series you watch, at the time they air where you are. It shares a row with Continue watching as a tab
- Newly popular: popular films, series and anime from the last 3, 6 or 12 months, best rated first. It shares a row with Your library as a tab and is on by default. Only new anime show, not later seasons, and titles you have seen stay out
- Every card can show its name, not only the focused one
- The Spotlight art eases back into place when you move, instead of snapping

### Screensaver
- The ambient screensaver shows art from what you are watching and what airs next, with the time, date, weather and the title in one corner
- The clock moves between the corners in a fixed order every few minutes instead of jumping at random
- With nothing to show, or no connection, it keeps to the clock and the colours

### Make it yours
- Six title fonts for the Spotlight
- Focus Style: white ring and lift, white ring, or accent glow
- Show in App Launcher: add Scene to the TV's own list of apps. It is off by default

### Smaller changes
- Sounds are on by default
- Sheets lead their title with an icon
- Now Playing keeps the time and temperature in view beside the song
- All apps spreads its columns edge to edge
